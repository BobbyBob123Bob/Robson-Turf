Lawn care guide

Getting your new lawn off to a great start

Most of the work happens before the turf arrives and in the first four weeks after.

  • Clear the area of old grass, weeds, rocks and debris. Spray persistent weeds a couple of weeks ahead.
  • Loosen the soil to a depth of roughly 100 mm so new roots can push down easily.
  • Add quality underlay such as a sandy loam turf underlay if your soil is heavy clay or very sandy.
  • Level and grade so water drains away from the house, and finish the soil about 25–30 mm below paths and edges.
  • Fertilise with a starter (pre-turf) fertiliser raked lightly into the surface.
  • Lightly dampen the soil the day before. Moist soil helps the roots, but don't leave it muddy.
  • Lay it the day it arrives. Turf is a living product, and rolls left stacked in the sun will heat up and yellow.
  • Start along a straight edge like a path or driveway, and work outward.
  • Stagger the joins in a brick pattern and butt the edges tightly together without overlapping or stretching.
  • Trim to fit with a sharp spade or old bread knife around curves and beds.
  • Roll or firmly pat the turf down so the roots make good contact with the soil.
  • Water straight away. Soak it until the soil underneath is wet, then lift a corner to check.
Week 1

Water once or twice daily so the turf and soil never dry out. Keep foot traffic off.

Week 2

Water every day or so, then start reducing as roots begin to grip.

Weeks 3–4

Water deeply every 2–3 days. Deep, less frequent watering trains roots to grow down.

First mow

Once the turf has rooted and you can't easily lift it (often 2–3 weeks), mow on a high setting and take off no more than a third of the leaf.

Hot, windy days need extra water. In cooler months turf establishes more slowly, so take it easy. If you're unsure, call us.

Our story

A Hunter family business, half a century in the making

Robson Turf began in the early 1970s, cutting turf from a farm near Hexham alongside the family's Robson's Bushland Nursery. As demand grew, the family bought land on the fertile Bolwarra Flats in late 1978, and that's where every roll of our turf has been grown ever since.

We're still a hands-on, local operation. We grow it, cut it fresh to order and deliver it ourselves, and we're happy to give you straight advice on what will work in your yard.

FAQ

Common questions

How much notice do you need for an order?

Give us a few days' notice where you can, especially in spring and summer when things get busy. We cut turf to order, so call us to check availability for your date.

Can I pick up turf from the farm?

Yes. Our farm is at 78 Flat Road, Bolwarra NSW 2320. Call ahead so we can have your order cut and ready. Remember that turf is heavy, so check your trailer or ute's load rating.

What time of year is best to lay turf?

Warm-season grasses like couch, kikuyu and buffalo establish fastest from spring through to early autumn. You can lay turf in the cooler months too. It just roots more slowly and needs less water.

How long can turf sit before it's laid?

Lay it the same day it's delivered. Rolled turf heats up quickly, especially in the sun, so the sooner it's down and watered the better.

Which areas do you deliver to?

Newcastle, Maitland, the wider Hunter Valley and the Central Coast. If you're outside these areas, give us a call and we'll see what we can do.
